BEFORE WE TALK

A few practical questions.

Start with one decision. The scope can grow when the evidence and the fit support it.

What does the first engagement look like?

A readiness diagnostic reviews one agreed product or technical concern. Typical outputs include ranked risks, evidence gaps, recommended tests, and a decision review. Scope, fees, availability, tools, and any specialist support are agreed before work begins.

Can you help with medical-device engineering?

Relevant experience includes verification and qualification support, test-method evaluation, laboratory coordination, and engineering documentation. Regulatory submissions, certification, and clinical claims require the appropriate responsible specialists and are separately scoped.

Do you support government R&D proposals?

Yes. Support can include the technical narrative, work packages, measurable milestones, development risks, schedule, and budget assumptions for a defined opportunity. Funding decisions remain with the sponsor.

Can you work with our team remotely or on site?

GeminiWave is based in Sudbury, Massachusetts. Remote collaboration is available across New England and beyond. Client-site visits depend on the agreed project, travel needs, schedule, and availability.

What should we share for a project fit call?

A high-level product description, the technical obstacle, your target milestone, and the decision you need to make. Keep confidential details out of the first inquiry; we can agree on an appropriate channel for further information.
