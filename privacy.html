Privacy and project inquiries

This website introduces Faheem Faheem’s engineering consulting services.

Information you enter

The project inquiry form prepares an email in your browser. It does not submit form fields to a GeminiWave server or save them in browser storage. You choose whether to send the prepared message using your own email application.

Email communication

If you send an inquiry, the information you provide is received through email and used to respond to your request and discuss potential work. Please do not include confidential technical information, patient records or controlled technical data in an initial inquiry. Share only information you are authorized to disclose.

Hosting and external services

The hosting provider may process technical request information to operate and secure the site. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts. External links and your email provider have their own privacy practices. This page does not add marketing analytics or advertising trackers.

Questions

For questions about an inquiry or to request deletion of an inquiry you sent, contact ffaheem@gmail.com.
